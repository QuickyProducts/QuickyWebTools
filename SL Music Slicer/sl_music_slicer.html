Three things and you know the tool

Click a song to select it, click it again to let it go, or hold the button and drag across several.

Double-click a song to open its editor and cut it. Land on its title and you are in the name field right away - that name is what your clips are called in Second Life. Esc closes the editor again.

Then Export at the bottom right: the songs you picked, or ALL of them in one go.
