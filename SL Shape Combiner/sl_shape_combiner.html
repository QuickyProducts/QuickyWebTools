SL Shape Combiner

Drop two exported shape XML files in - the one whose face you want and the one whose body you want. The tool builds one shape from both, ready to import back into your viewer. Everything stays on your computer - nothing is uploaded.

How does it work?

You have one shape with a face you love, and another shape with a body you love. This tool glues them together: face from one, body from the other. You never have to copy sliders by hand.

Step 0 - turn on the Develop menu (one time only). The export lives in a hidden Firestorm menu. Press Ctrl-Alt-Q - a new menu called Develop appears in the top menu bar. (You can also go to Avatar → Preferences → Advanced and tick "Show the Develop menu".)

Step 1 - save both shapes as files. Wear the shape with the good face. In the top menu bar click Develop → Avatar → Character Tests → Appearance To XML and save the file, for example as head.xml. Now wear the shape with the good body and do exactly the same again, saving it as body.xml. You end up with two files on your computer.

Step 2 - drop them in above. The face file goes into the left box (Head Shape), the body file into the right box (Body Shape).

Step 3 - click Combine. Your browser saves one new file. That's your mixed shape. Face, eyes, eyebrows, ears, nose, mouth and chin come from the left file; height, body, arms, chest, belly, hips, butt, legs and feet come from the right file. It happens automatically - there is nothing to configure.

Step 4 - get it back into Second Life. In your inventory, right-click a folder → New Body Parts → New Shape. Wear that new shape (you will look like a default avatar for a moment - that's fine). Then Edit Shape → Import XML, pick the file from step 3, and click Save As. Done - your old shapes stay untouched.

Nothing is uploaded anywhere. The two files are read right here in your browser, and the result is saved straight to your computer.
